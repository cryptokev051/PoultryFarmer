Starting a poultry farm

Plan the farm before buying birds

If you are researching how to start poultry farming in Kenya, begin with the production model, target customer, suitable site and number of birds you can finance and manage. Prepare housing, water, feed storage, brooding and health routines before chicks arrive.

Costs & profitability

Understand the full cost of production

Assess initial investment, housing, feed, chicks or breeding stock, flock health, labour and other management expenses. Compare those costs with realistic selling prices, market access and working-capital needs. Break-even matters more than an unsupported profit promise.
