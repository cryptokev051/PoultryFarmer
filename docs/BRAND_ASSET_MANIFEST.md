## Excluded from the theme copy

The other 34 files remain preserved in `brand-assets/` and are excluded from the installable theme: all `00_Start_Here` documents, the approved reference and print/background primary exports, non-selected lockups, separate tagline/wordmark exports, duplicate icon sizes and Android-named duplicates, all social media exports, the email signature, and all monochrome/grayscale variants. Guidelines and support files (`PDF`, `CSV`, `JSON`, `TXT`) are not theme runtime dependencies.
